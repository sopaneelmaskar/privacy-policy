Simple ADAS Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Simple ADAS driver-assistance app for iPhone (“the app”), published by Sopaneel Maskar (“we”, “us”), handles information.

  • Camera images and sensor readings are analysed on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • The only information the app sends on its own is an approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, used to fetch local weather.
  • Optional recordings are off by default, stay on your phone, and leave it only if you share them yourself.

Camera

The rear camera is used to detect vehicles, pedestrians, lane markings and traffic lights ahead of you. The front camera is used only for the optional driver alertness check, which runs while the vehicle is stopped. Camera images are processed on your phone as they arrive and then discarded. They are not uploaded.

Images are saved only if you turn on one of the recording options described below.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location while it is open. Your speed is used on the phone to decide when warnings should be given.

To adjust visibility advisories for local weather, the app sends your location, rounded to about 1 km, to the Open-Meteo weather service. The app also sends Open-Meteo a request with no location to check whether the phone is online. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo receives your device’s IP address. These requests contain no name, account or device identifier. See Open-Meteo’s privacy policy.

If you decline location access, the app still works and tells you that speed is unavailable.

Motion sensors

Accelerometer and gyroscope readings are used on the phone to estimate the phone’s orientation, your lane position and sudden braking. They are not sent anywhere.

Optional recordings

These features are off unless you turn them on in Settings:

  • Drive Trace Recorder saves detection results, sensor readings and timing, and can include small scene images.
  • Evaluation Clip Mode saves up to 20 seconds of rear-camera video alongside a trace.
  • Incident capture and photo on alert save a short record or photo around a warning.

Recordings are stored only inside the app on your phone. They leave the phone only if you share them yourself with the share button, and then go wherever you choose to send them. You can delete them in Settings, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Settings

Your preferences, calibration values and the last weather result are stored on your phone so the app works offline.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising services
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

Because we receive no data from the app, we hold no personal information about you to access or delete. You can remove data from your phone by deleting recordings in Settings or deleting the app.

Children

Simple ADAS is a driving aid intended for adult drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of the app sends more information off your phone, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
