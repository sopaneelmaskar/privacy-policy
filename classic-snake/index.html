Snake Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Snake game for iPhone and iPad (“the app”), published by Sopaneel Maskar (“we”, “us”), handles information. It also covers the web version of the game.

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or other personal details to play.
  • Your scores and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • We do not run our own analytics or track you across other apps and websites.
  • The iOS app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google receives some technical information to deliver those ads, as described below.
  • The web version has no ads, and the game itself sends no information anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves the following on your device:

  • Your settings: controls, walls, speed, maze and sound.
  • Your five best scores, with the mode, maze and date of each run.
  • Whether you have played before, so a new player’s first ad waits longer.
  • When an ad-free period you earned by watching a rewarded video ends.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising

The iOS app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. Ads appear only between games, never during a run. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video to turn off these ads for 30 minutes.

What Google receives

To request, show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app sends Google technical information such as:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • a device identifier that is specific to this app’s publisher (the identifier for vendors), along with device type, operating system and app version;
  • information about the ads shown to you and how you interact with them, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic and performance information about ad loading.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google’s use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Non-personalized ads and tracking

The app asks Google for non-personalized ads, which are not based on your past behavior in other apps or websites. The app does not ask for permission to track you, so your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ads are requested. Ads are loaded only if that form allows it. When it applies to you, you can change your choices at any time from Ad privacy choices on the game’s main menu.

Reporting an ad

The main menu includes a link to report an inappropriate ad. It opens your email app with a message addressed to us. If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and we use them only to look into your report and reply.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash reporting services.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location services, camera or microphone.

Playing offline

The game works without a network connection. While you are offline, no ads are requested and no information is sent.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app at any time to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • Change your ad consent choices from Ad privacy choices on the main menu, where available.
  • Reset your device’s identifier for vendors by deleting the app (and any other apps from the same publisher) and reinstalling it.
  • For information Google holds about you, use Google’s privacy controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
