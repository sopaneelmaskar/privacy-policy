Lantern Loop Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Lantern Loop game for iPhone and iPad (“the app”), published by Sopaneel Maskar (“we”, “us”), handles information. It also covers the web version of the game.

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or other personal details to play.
  • Your progress, settings and statistics are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • We do not run our own analytics or crash reporting, and the game has no in-app purchases.
  • The iOS app shows only rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one. Google receives some information to deliver those ads, as described below.
  • You decide whether the app may track you. Saying no never affects gameplay or rewards.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, it saves the following on your device:

  • Your settings: music and sound volume, screen shake and reduced flashing.
  • Your progress: shards, unlocked characters and perks, and your best Daily Night score.
  • Your statistics: number of runs, best night reached, best score, total play time, and whether you have finished your first run (so the tips are shown only once).
  • A gameplay log used to improve the game’s difficulty: when play sessions and runs start and end, when and how each loop ended, and which nights were cleared. It holds at most 2,000 entries, removing the oldest first.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. We only see the gameplay log if you choose to export it yourself and send it to us. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. In the web version, the same information is kept in your browser’s local storage, and clearing the site’s data removes it.

Advertising

The iOS app is free and supported by rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. An ad plays only when you tap a button that offers a reward, such as re-rolling your upgrade choices or doubling the shards earned in a run. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. If no ad is available, the reward button is hidden or disabled.

What Google receives

To request, show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app sends Google technical information such as:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • a device identifier that is specific to this app’s publisher (the identifier for vendors), along with device type, operating system and app version;
  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • information about the ads shown to you and how you interact with them, such as views, taps and whether a video was completed;
  • diagnostic and performance information about ad loading.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google’s use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Tracking permission

Before the first ad is requested, iOS asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. If you allow it, Google may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you and to measure them. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the app requests only non-personalized ads, which are not based on your past behavior in other apps or websites, and your advertising identifier is not available to the app or to Google. You can change this at any time in your device’s Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking. Your choice never affects gameplay or rewards.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ads are requested. Ads are loaded only if that form allows it. When it applies to you, you can change your choices at any time from Ad privacy choices in the game’s menu.

US state privacy laws

Some US state laws treat personalized advertising as “sharing” personal information. We do not sell your personal information. To opt out of personalized ads, choose “Ask App Not to Track” when iOS asks, turn off tracking for the app in Settings, or use Ad privacy choices in the game’s menu where it is shown.

Content of ads

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Web version

When you play Lantern Loop on a web games site, that site operates the page you are on and may show its own ads. Its privacy policy applies to the information it collects. The game itself does not send any information to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash reporting services.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location services, camera or microphone.
  • We do not offer accounts or in-app purchases.

Playing offline

The game works without a network connection. While you are offline, no ads are requested, no information is sent, and the reward buttons are unavailable.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app at any time to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • Allow or stop tracking for the app at any time in your device’s Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Change your ad consent choices from Ad privacy choices in the game’s menu, where available.
  • For information Google holds about you, use Google’s privacy controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
